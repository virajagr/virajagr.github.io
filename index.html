I am a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research India, where I work with Kirankumar Shiragur and Ankit Garg. I am interested in the algorithmic foundations of retrieval: what a vector representation can and cannot encode, and how to search over such representations at scale. We built a family of retrieval tasks where ranking the relevant documents above the irrelevant ones requires exponentially large single-vector embeddings, while polynomial-size multi-vector embeddings suffice, and turned the construction into a benchmark, ANDOR.

I also work with Ravishankar Krishnaswamy and Magdalen Dobson Manohar on approximate nearest neighbor search where a query carries attribute filters alongside its vector, extending the DiskANN line of work.

Before Microsoft Research, I spent a year as a quantitative researcher at Quantbox Research, looking for short-horizon predictive signals in Indian equity and index-options markets. Throughout, competitive programming has been a long-standing habit: I competed for about four years, represented IIT Delhi at the ICPC Asia West Finals 2024, and eventually spent as much time setting and testing problems as solving them.

I will be applying to PhD programs and am also open to research engineer roles.

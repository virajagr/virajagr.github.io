Competitive Programming

Competitive programming is where I learned to think about algorithms. I competed for about four years and represented IIT Delhi at the ICPC Asia West Finals 2024.

Over time I moved to the other side of it, setting and testing problems across Codeforces, CodeChef (as a member of the contest admin team), ICPC Regionals 2025-26, and the Algorithms and Coding Club at IIT Delhi.

Profiles: Codeforces, CodeChef, AtCoder, ICPC.

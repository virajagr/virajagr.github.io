  • Retrieval Needs Multivectors: An Exponential Separation
    Mihir Agarwal, Viraj Agrawal, Sabyasachi Basu, Ankit Garg, Kirankumar Shiragur.
    arXiv:2608.21494, August 2026. [pdf] [bibtex]
    Summary

    Multi-vector embeddings consistently outperform single-vector ones in retrieval, but the representational gap between them was poorly understood. Following Jayaram's work, we give the first explicit family of query and document sets, together with their relevance matrices, for which single-vector embeddings that rank all relevant documents above irrelevant ones require exponential size, whereas polynomial-size multi-vector embeddings suffice. This establishes an exponential separation in expressive power for the task of ranking documents. Motivated by the construction, we introduce ANDOR, a benchmark that naturally instantiates these hard examples: state-of-the-art single-vector models perform poorly zero-shot and improve only marginally with fine-tuning, while multi-vector models consistently do better and improve substantially with fine-tuning, closely matching our theoretical predictions.
